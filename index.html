“In my opinion there is nothing on the market that compares to Mini Crotchets.”

Founded by Joya Logan, GTCL LTCL, Mini Crotchets introduces young children aged 6 months to 3 years to the enjoyment of music. Established in 1992, it became a leading toddler music group in South London, and relocated to Ropley, Hampshire in September 2024.

Classes emphasise enjoyment through music — action songs, counting songs, traditional nursery rhymes, finger‑puppet activities and instrument playing. Children develop concentration, coordination, sharing and the foundational skills needed before starting school.

Sessions are interactive and educational, featuring Joya's singing accompanied by live piano with choreographed actions. Classes are kept small to create an intimate, engaging atmosphere with mixed‑age groups. Each session lasts 30 minutes and requires an accompanying adult.

Parties

Mini Crotchet parties

“Wow, Live music! I have been to others with backing tracks and it's not the same!”

A Mini Crotchet Party is a lovely way to entertain children: Joya performs a half‑hour musical programme of well‑known children's songs, ending with “Happy Birthday”. The party includes keyboard accompaniment, musical instruments, finger puppets and interactive fun. Best suited to children aged 2–4 years, though flexible.
