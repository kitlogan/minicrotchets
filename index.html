About the class

Genuinely musical, wonderfully gentle

“In my opinion there is nothing on the market that compares to Mini Crotchets.”

— Joya Logan

Founded by Joya Logan (GTCL LTCL) in 1992 and now in Ropley, Mini Crotchets introduces children aged 6 months to 3 years to the joy of music — action songs, nursery rhymes, finger puppets and real instruments.

Every session is led by Joya's singing and live piano: small, gentle and genuinely musical. 30 minutes, with a grown-up alongside.
